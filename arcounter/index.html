Feature Suite

All-in-One Smart Counting Assistant

Engineered for speed, accuracy, and versatility across warehouse management, manufacturing, logistics, and field audits.

1. Real-Time AR Live Camera

Point your camera at everyday objects and let on-device AI detect and highlight items instantly in real time.

  • 60–120 Hz smooth overlay interpolation
  • Tap individual items or tap "Add All"
  • Built-in flashlight/torch for dark warehouses
  • 3×3 framing grid & manual EV exposure

2. Virtual Tripwire & ROI Zones

Set automated directional counting lines and custom regions of interest to count objects moving along conveyor belts.

  • Automatic IN / OUT directional counting
  • Perfect for conveyors, doorways, turnstiles
  • 4 ROI Zones: Center, Strip, Floor, Gate
  • Live cadence tracker (items per minute)

3. Dense Photo Inventory AI

Snap a photo or import high-res gallery images to count hundreds of repeating objects in seconds with specialized algorithms.

  • Dense Circle / Blob Counter (timber, pipes, pills)
  • Vertical Stalk & Rod morphological mode
  • Manual add/remove with instant Undo / Redo
  • Boxes, Numbered Badges (#1, #2), or Dots

4. Multi-Board Tactile Tally Clicker

Sleek digital dial with haptic vibration feedback for manual high-speed counting with concurrent multi-category boards.

  • Track "Pass", "Defect", "Reject", "Box A" simultaneously
  • Physical Volume Up/Down button counting
  • Goal tracking with target threshold alerts
  • Custom color-coded tags and category names

5. PDF Reports & CSV Export

Generate formal audit-ready documentation and raw data export for seamless ERP, Excel, and spreadsheet integration.

  • Formal PDF reports with annotated photo snapshots
  • Session notes, timestamps, and category breakdowns
  • CSV export (Session summaries & granular timestamps)
  • Native Android System Share Sheet

6. Searchable Session Library

Every counting session is saved locally in an organized, searchable SQLite database sandboxed on your device.

  • Filter by mode: Live AR, Photo AI, Manual Tally
  • Search by session title, tags, or notes
  • Inspect historical snapshots and audit logs
  • One-tap "Clear All History" privacy control
Computer Vision Technology

Deep-Dive: AR Scanning & Dense Photo AI

Combining Google MediaPipe, TensorFlow Lite, and custom morphological filters for unmatched mobile accuracy.

Live AR Camera

Real-Time Detection & Tripwires

Point your device at items moving across a factory table or conveyor line. The on-device neural engine detects items and renders floating circular badges with 60–120 Hz interpolation.

  • Directional Tripwire: Set virtual line barriers across doorways or conveyors. The app detects crossing vectors and automatically tallies IN or OUT counts.
  • ROI Zones: Constrain analysis to specific screen regions (Center, Conveyor Strip, Floor/Table, Gate) to filter out background movements.
  • Cadence Tracker: Displays live items-per-minute throughput metrics.
Dense Photo AI

Timber, Pipes, Bundles & Pills

Counting dense cross-sections by hand is prone to fatigue and human error. AR Camera Counter's dedicated Hough circle transform and blob detection algorithms isolate repeating items in seconds.

  • Dense Circle / Blob Mode: Tuned for timber logs, steel pipes, rebar rods, metal cylinders, pills, and produce.
  • Vertical Stalk & Rod Mode: Morphological algorithms for upright bars and seedlings.
  • Interactive Editing: Tap any missed item to add a dot; tap any false positive to remove it. Instant Undo & Redo.
  • 500% Pinch Zoom: Deep zoom to verify dense regions with 1-tap reset.
Manual Tally

Multi-Board Tactile Tally Clicker

Need to audit multiple categories at once? Multi-Board Mode gives you independent digital counters on a single screen with haptic pulse feedback.

  • Concurrent Categories: Track "Pass", "Defect", "Reject", or custom batches simultaneously.
  • Physical Volume Buttons: Count without looking by pressing your device's Volume Up / Volume Down keys.
  • Goal Thresholds: Set target quantities and get visual & haptic notifications upon reaching them.
Audit Documentation

Formal PDF Reports & CSV Integration

Turn counting sessions into official audit documents ready for supervisors, clients, and inventory databases.

  • PDF Inspection Reports: Includes high-resolution annotated photo snapshots, item counts, category totals, operator notes, and timestamps.
  • Dual CSV Export: Exports both high-level session summaries and granular per-item event timestamps for ERP & Excel import.
  • System Share Sheet: One-tap sharing via email, WhatsApp, Google Drive, or Slack.

Permission Transparency

Clear & Honest Permission Usage

AR Camera Counter requests only the minimal runtime permissions required to function. Here is our full disclosure:

Permission Technical Android Name Functional Purpose
Camera android.permission.CAMERA Used solely to stream video frames to the on-device AI detector for real-time object counting and to capture inventory snapshot photos. Video frames are processed in volatile memory and are never transmitted over the internet.
Vibration android.permission.VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic vibration feedback when an object is counted, clicked, or when a counting goal threshold is achieved.
Internet android.permission.INTERNET Used solely by third-party services (Google AdMob for non-intrusive advertisements and Google Play In-App Updates to check for new versions).
Network State android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used by Google AdMob to verify network connectivity before requesting advertisements, preventing unnecessary background retries when offline.
