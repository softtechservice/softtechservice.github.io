Legal Information & Policies

Effective Date: July 4, 2026 | Developer Team: Antigravity Games

Bubble Vibe Shooter is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle data within our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Sharing

Bubble Vibe Shooter does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) from our players. The game runs locally on your device and does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any email addresses, phone numbers, or personal profile data.

2. Device Local Storage

The app uses your device's local sandbox storage (Web Storage API / SharedPreferences API) to save and retain gameplay progress:

  • Unlocked levels
  • Game stats (high scores, games played, bubbles popped)
  • Coin counts and power-up inventory (Fireballs, Bombs, Rainbows)
  • Claimed daily rewards history

Data Retention: This data remains completely on your physical device. Uninstalling the application or clearing the application cache will remove this data. We do not sync, back up, or transmit this progress to external cloud environments.

3. Permissions & Device Integration

The App requests the following system permissions to improve your gaming experience:

Permission Purpose
Internet Access Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load advertisement scripts.
Haptic/Vibration Feedback Used to trigger physical device vibrations during pops and level completion events.
Local Notifications Used to schedule daily alerts locally (at 7:00 PM) reminding you to claim daily coins. This does not connect to external push servers.

4. Third-Party Services (AdMob)

This app displays advertisements powered by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). AdMob may collect certain device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate geolocation, or anonymous analytics data for ad targeting, frequency capping, and performance analytics.
