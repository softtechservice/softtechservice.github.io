Include your app version, phone model, Android version and the steps that led to the issue. For account help, write from your app sign-in email. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, government ID documents or full payment-card details.

Pro and purchases

I paid, but Pro has not unlocked.

Check that the payment completed in Google Play and that you are using the original app account and Play account. Connect to the internet and choose Restore purchases. Pending payments need to complete before Pro can be verified. If the order is completed and restore still fails, contact support with the GPA order number.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Cancel through Google Play subscriptions. Requesting a refund is a separate process and does not happen simply by uninstalling. Follow our refund and cancellation instructions.

Can I move Pro to another phone?

Use the same app sign-in and Google Play account and choose Restore purchases. Cloud restoration requires supported records to have been synced to your account. Device-only dashcam files and exported copies need separate transfer.

Location, trips and circles

Why does GPS speed or my route look wrong?

GPS can be inaccurate or delayed indoors, in tunnels, near tall buildings or with a weak signal. Check location permissions and precise-location access. Use your vehicle's instruments and posted road signs; the app is an estimate, not a certified measurement tool.

Why did background tracking stop?

Check the enabled tracking feature, location permissions and your phone's battery settings for the app. Android and device manufacturers may limit background operation. Review permissions and background-location details before changing access.

How do I stop sharing my location?

Pause sharing in each circle or leave the circle. Disable geofences and automatic trips separately if you no longer want those location features. Stopping one trip does not turn every background feature off. Previously shared entries can remain until their cleanup period.

Why is cloud sync unavailable?

Cloud sync needs a signed-in account, Pro access and connectivity. Check these first. Ending Pro pauses sync but does not automatically remove an existing cloud backup. Read how cloud data is handled.

Accounts and privacy

I cannot sign in.

Check the email address used to create the app account. Use the password-reset option on the sign-in screen if needed. If you still need help, contact support from that email address. Never send your password or a reset link to support.

How do I delete my account or cloud data?

Use Delete Account in the app's Account screen, or follow the web account-deletion instructions. You can also request deletion of particular cloud records without deleting the account. Cancel any Play subscription separately if you want renewals to stop.

Are dashcam recordings uploaded automatically?

Dashcam video and audio stay on your phone unless you choose to export or share them. Cloud sync does not automatically upload these recordings. See the privacy policy for other online features.
