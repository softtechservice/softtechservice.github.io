Privacy Policy

SpeedoMeter Pro (com.softtechservice.speedometerpro) is provided by SOFTTECH SERVICE. This policy explains how the app uses your information and the choices available to you.

Local first, with online features: Driving records are stored on your phone. Account services, Pro cloud backup, family or employee circles, purchase verification, ads and optional crash reporting use online services. Joining a circle or using cloud sync can send data automatically while those features are enabled; uploads do not require a manual export each time.

1. Driving records and device permissions

GPS coordinates, speed, altitude, bearing, timestamps and motion sensors support the speedometer, route recording, driving events, accident/fall detection and alerts. Trips, vehicles, fuel and service records are kept in a local encrypted database. Settings and configured emergency contacts are also stored locally.

Background location: When enabled, automatic trips, geofences and circle sharing can process location even when the app is closed or not in use. A foreground-service notification is shown during trip recording. Ending a trip does not disable separately enabled circle sharing, automatic trip detection or geofences. Pause or disable each feature, or revoke its location permission, to stop that use.

Activity recognition detects vehicle entry/exit for automatic trips. Optional Bluetooth access connects an OBD-II adapter. Notifications provide tracking status, driving alerts and circle events. The floating dashboard requires permission to display over other apps.

2. Dashcam, exports and emergency messages

The dashcam uses the camera and, if permitted and enabled, microphone. Recordings are saved on your phone and are not automatically uploaded by cloud sync; video files are separate from the encrypted database. Rolling recording can replace older unprotected clips. You control exports and sharing with other apps or people, and those recipients may retain copies.

If you configure emergency contacts and enable SOS, the app can send your location and an emergency message through your carrier SMS service when you trigger SOS or an enabled detection countdown completes. Your carrier and the recipients process these messages; carrier charges may apply. These features are not a substitute for emergency services.

3. Accounts and Pro cloud backup

Firebase Authentication processes your email address and sign-in credentials. Your app account ID, email, account profile and activity timestamps are stored with Google Firebase to provide account services. Local onboarding profile fields are separate from a display name you choose to share in a circle.

When signed in with Pro access, cloud sync can automatically upload vehicles, trips, route coordinates/tracking points, geofences and fuel records to your account in Firebase Firestore. This supports backup and restoration on another device. Signing out or losing Pro pauses sync; it does not automatically delete existing cloud backups.

4. Optional family and employee circles

Creating or joining a circle shares information with its members: your display name, latest location, accuracy, speed, heading, battery level, driving state, trip summaries (distance, duration, speeds and driving-event counts, without the full route), and arrival, departure or speeding events. Circle places and geofence coordinates are also stored in Firebase. Cloud Messaging uses a device push token linked to your account to deliver circle alerts.

Use circles only with informed consent. You can pause sharing per circle or leave. Pausing stops new updates; a previously shared position or event can remain visible. Leaving removes your member position and trip summaries through server cleanup. Recent event entries can remain until scheduled deletion. A circle owner can manage membership; do not share an invite code with unintended people.

5. Subscriptions and purchase verification

Google Play handles checkout and payments. SOFTTECH SERVICE does not receive your full card or bank details. Our Firebase backend stores purchase tokens, product IDs, subscription state, expiry and verification timestamps linked to your app account to verify purchases, restore access, prevent token reuse and process renewal/refund notifications. A hashed app account identifier is supplied to Google Play to bind purchases. Circle members may see whether the owner's plan is active, without their payment details.

6. Ads, diagnostics and service providers

The free version displays Google AdMob ads. Google may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information under its policies and applicable consent choices. Consent and privacy-options controls are provided where required and available. Pro removes in-app ads.

Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in the updated app. Crash reporting is off by default. If you enable it in Settings, Firebase Crashlytics receives crash traces, app/OS/device details and diagnostic identifiers to investigate failures. Reports are not guaranteed to be anonymous. Turning it off stops future uploads and deletes unsent reports on your device; reports already delivered are subject to Google's retention practices.

Google Play services provides location, activity recognition, billing and app-integrity services. Online maps request tiles from OpenStreetMap; tile requests disclose the requested map area and network information such as your IP address. Weather requests send coordinates to Open-Meteo. These providers may process network/service logs to deliver and protect their services. We do not sell your personal data. Information is disclosed to providers to run these features, to circle members or recipients you choose, or where legally required.

7. Retention, deletion and security

Local records remain until you delete them, apply an automatic deletion setting, clear app storage or uninstall. Files exported outside the app and data already synced to the cloud are separate copies. Clearing the app cache does not delete your account or all saved records.

Cloud backup and purchase-verification records are retained to provide the account services until account deletion. Circle event history is scheduled for cleanup after 30 days; cleanup runs periodically, so removal need not occur at the exact cutoff. Leaving a circle removes your membership, location and member trip summaries; deleting an owned circle starts cleanup of its shared records.

Account deletion removes your Firebase sign-in account, cloud backup, subscription verification records and stored push tokens, and triggers removal of your membership/location and circles you own. Some cleanup is asynchronous. Google retains its own Play orders and service records under its policies and legal requirements. Cancelling a subscription does not itself erase records, and deleting an account does not cancel billing.

The app uses encrypted local database storage, HTTPS for online services and account access rules for cloud records. Exported files and dashcam video are separate from the encrypted database. Google services may process information outside your country. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and children

You can change permissions in Android Settings, disable automatic trips or geofences, pause/leave circles, turn crash reporting off, export/delete supported records and request account deletion. Denying a permission can make its associated feature unavailable. This app is not directed at children under 13; a parent or guardian should manage any circle involving a young person. Contact us about a child's information or any request for access, correction or deletion.

We may update this policy as features change. The date above identifies this version.

Refunds and cancellation

Stop future renewals

  1. Open the Play Store using the Google account that purchased Pro.
  2. Choose Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → SpeedoMeter Pro.
  3. Choose Cancel subscription and follow Google's confirmation steps.

You can also open Google Play subscriptions or tap Manage subscription in the app. Cancelling normally keeps Pro until the paid period ends. Uninstalling the app or deleting your app account does not cancel billing.

Request a refund

Use Google Play order history to locate the order and report a problem/request a refund where available, or follow Google Play's refund instructions. Eligibility and processing depend on Google's policy, the purchase and applicable consumer law; refunds are not automatic.

For purchase support, email contect@softtechservice.site with the Google Play order number (beginning GPA.) and a description of the issue. Do not send card details, bank credentials or passwords. Refunds, where approved, are handled through Google Play. This policy does not limit statutory consumer rights.

After expiry or entitlement removal

Paid features and sync stop when the verified entitlement expires or is revoked. Your local data remains. A refund and cancellation are separate actions; check the subscription's status in Google Play.

Delete your account and data

This applies to SpeedoMeter Pro (com.softtechservice.speedometerpro) by SOFTTECH SERVICE.

Cancel Pro in Google Play first if you want renewals to stop. Account deletion does not cancel your Play subscription or automatically refund a payment. See cancellation instructions.

From the app

  2. Open the Account screen and choose Delete Account.
  3. Review the confirmation and confirm permanent deletion.

What is removed

  • The Firebase sign-in account and account profile.
  • Your synced vehicles, trips, route points, geofences and fuel logs.
  • Our subscription verification records, account-linked purchase-token records and stored push tokens.
  • Your circle membership and member location/trip summaries, and circles you own, through server cleanup.

Some server cleanup runs asynchronously. Recent circle events can remain until scheduled cleanup after 30 days. Google service logs/backups follow Google's retention practices; deletion does not remove screenshots, exports or copies already kept by other recipients.

What needs separate action

Local phone data, dashcam videos and exported files require separate deletion in the app, Android app-storage controls or the location where you exported them. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes app-private local data, not prior exports or cloud accounts. Google Play retains its own order records and subscriptions under its policies. Manage or cancel your Play subscription separately.
