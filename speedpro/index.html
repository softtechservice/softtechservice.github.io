Before you subscribe

Pro, explained.

How do I upgrade or restore Pro?

Open SpeedoMeter Pro on Android, sign in to your app account and choose Upgrade to Pro. Select monthly or yearly, then review and confirm the Google Play checkout. To recover an existing purchase, use the original app account and Google Play account, then choose Restore purchases. Pending payments unlock Pro only after the purchase is completed and verified.

How do I stop my subscription renewing?

Choose Manage subscription in the app, or open Google Play subscriptions with the account that purchased Pro. Cancel the SpeedoMeter Pro subscription and follow the confirmation steps. Access normally continues until the paid period ends. Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel billing. See refunds and cancellation for purchase support.

What happens when Pro ends?

Paid features and cloud sync stop, and the free app continues with ads. Your local records are not automatically deleted. Cancelling does not erase existing cloud backups; use the account and data deletion options if you want those removed.
