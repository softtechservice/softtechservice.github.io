1. Safety and permitted use

Set up the app before driving, mount the phone safely and obey local laws. GPS and sensor readings are estimates and may be inaccurate or unavailable. Do not rely on the app as a certified instrument or emergency service. You remain responsible for driving, speed limits and lawful recording. Read the driving and GPS disclaimer.

2. Accounts and circles

Protect your sign-in details and circle invite codes. Only share or monitor locations with the person's informed consent. Employers must meet applicable workplace-monitoring requirements. Do not access another person's data without authorization, interfere with the service or use it unlawfully.

3. Pro plans and payment

Pro is offered as monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions. Current India prices are ₹99 per month and ₹999 per year. No lifetime plan is offered for sale and no free trial is currently offered. Google Play displays the final currency, price, taxes and billing terms before you confirm payment. Other regions or future offers, if available, use the terms shown at checkout.

Google Play processes payments and renews the subscription automatically until cancelled. Pro includes paid reports, travel allowance, earnings, deep timeline, dashcam export, cloud backup/sync and ad removal; feature availability is shown in the app. An eligible circle owner's active plan can provide inherited access to members. Members lose that inherited access when it is no longer eligible.

4. Cancellation, restoration and expiry

Use Manage subscription in the app or Google Play subscriptions to cancel renewal. Access normally remains until the paid period ends, unless refunded or revoked. Uninstalling or deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Restore purchases uses the original app account and Google Play account. Pending or unverified payments do not grant Pro.

When Pro ends, paid features and cloud sync stop. Local records are not automatically deleted. Refund requests follow the refund and cancellation instructions.
