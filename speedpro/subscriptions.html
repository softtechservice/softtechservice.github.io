Choose your billing period

Pro Monthly and Pro Yearly include the same Pro features. Purchases are made in the Android app through Google Play.

₹99 / month

Billed every month in India.

₹999 / year

Billed every year in India.

No free trial or lifetime plan is currently offered. Google Play displays the final price, currency, taxes and billing terms before you confirm payment.

What Pro includes

  • Advanced reports, travel allowance and earnings tools.
  • Deep timeline and dashcam export.
  • Cloud backup and sync for supported records.
  • Removal of in-app ads.
  • Family or employee circles, circle places and alerts, and member trip summaries.

The free app includes GPS speed, HUD and basic trip history with ads. Members can inherit eligible Pro access while their circle owner's plan is active.

Upgrade and restore

  1. Open SpeedoMeter Pro on Android and sign in to your app account.
  2. Choose Upgrade to Pro and select monthly or yearly.
  3. Review Google Play's checkout, then confirm if you want to buy.

To recover an existing plan, use the original app account and Google Play account, then choose Restore purchases. An internet connection is needed for verification. Pending or declined payments do not grant Pro; an expired or revoked purchase does not restore an active plan.

Renewal and cancellation

Both plans renew automatically until cancelled in Google Play. Use Manage subscription in the app or Google Play subscriptions. Cancellation normally leaves access until the paid period ends. Refund or revocation can end access earlier.

Uninstalling or deleting your app account does not cancel the Play subscription. See refunds and cancellation.

When Pro ends

Paid features and cloud sync stop, and the free app continues with ads. Local records are not automatically deleted. Cancelling does not erase existing cloud backups; use account and data deletion to request their removal.
