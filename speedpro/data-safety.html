SpeedoMeter Pro for Android
com.softtechservice.speedometerpro

This summarizes how the app handles information. Collection and sharing depend on the features you use. Read the full privacy policy for providers, retention and your choices.

DataProcessing and purpose
Location and driving activityProcessed locally for speed and trips; routes and records may sync to your account with Pro. Enabled circles share latest location and summaries with members.
Account informationEmail, account ID and account profile are processed by Firebase for sign-in and account services.
Purchase recordsPurchase tokens, product/entitlement state and timestamps support Google Play verification and restoration. We do not receive full payment-card details.
Device identifiers and diagnosticsPush tokens deliver circle alerts; AdMob may process identifiers and interactions for ads. Optional Crashlytics reports contain diagnostics. Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in the updated build.
Camera, audio and emergency contactsDashcam files and configured contacts stay local unless you share them. Enabled SOS sends location and an emergency message by carrier SMS.
DeletionDelete local records separately from cloud data. Use the Account screen or email deletion request for account/cloud deletion. Cancelling Pro does not delete data.

Control what you share

Change permissions in Android Settings, pause circle sharing, disable automatic trips or geofences, and choose whether to send crash reports. Ending a recorded trip does not automatically stop separately enabled circle sharing.
