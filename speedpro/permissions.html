Background-location choices

SpeedoMeter Pro uses background location for enabled automatic trips, location alerts/geofences and circle location sharing, even when the app is closed or not in use. Circle members can see updates shared with their circle. Disable these features separately or revoke background location in Android Settings to stop background use. Stopping a recorded trip alone does not turn them all off.

Change a permission

Open Android Settings, find SpeedoMeter Pro under Apps, and open Permissions. Labels vary by Android version and phone. Revoke or grant access there; a feature may stop working if its required permission is denied.

To stop new circle updates, pause sharing or leave the circle in the app. To stop automatic trip detection or geofence alerts, disable those features separately. See the privacy policy for information that can leave your phone.
