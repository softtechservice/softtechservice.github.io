Android Device Permissions Explained

Magic Fluids follows Android best practices by adhering to the principle of least privilege. Permissions are requested only when required for specific optional features.

Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)
Optional • Off by Default

Purpose: Required exclusively for the optional "Music Mode", enabling fluid color waves to pulse in synchrony with music playing on your phone.

Privacy Safeguard: The audio signal is processed through an in-memory Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) algorithm solely for frequency and beat detection. Audio is never recorded, never written to disk, and never transmitted over any network. Analysis terminates immediately when the live wallpaper is hidden.

Photos & Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)
Optional • User Initiated

Purpose: Allows you to pick a picture to extract color palettes or display your photo behind the fluid ("Photo Fluid").

Privacy Safeguard: Uses Android's modern photo picker where you grant access exclusively to the specific image you select. The image is processed locally and never uploaded to any remote server.

Motion Sensors (ACCELEROMETER / GYROSCOPE)
Standard Sensor Access

Purpose: Allows the real-time fluid simulation to respond to physical device tilting and orientation changes, making the liquid slosh naturally.

Privacy Safeguard: Motion vectors are fed directly into the GPU shader math and are not recorded or logged.

Battery Status Broadcasts
Standard Broadcast

Purpose: Enables the charging fluid animation and activates battery-saver frame throttling when your phone's battery is critically low.

Live Wallpaper Service (BIND_WALLPAPER)
Core Framework Service

Purpose: Android standard framework permission allowing Magic Fluids to render its real-time graphics on your system Home screen and Lock screen.
