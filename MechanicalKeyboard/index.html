Easy Setup

GET STARTED in 3 Simple Steps

Open the app, follow the setup steps to enable Mechanical Keyboard in Android settings, then select it as your keyboard. You can switch keyboards again in device settings anytime.

1

Open Companion App

Launch Mechanical Keyboard from your home screen or app drawer. The companion setup assistant will guide you through permissions and quick setup.

2

Enable in Android Settings

Tap Enable in Settings to open Android's Manage on-screen keyboards menu. Toggle on Mechanical Keyboard and confirm Android's standard prompt.

3

Select & Personalize

Select Mechanical Keyboard as your active input method. You are now ready to customize RGB themes, switch acoustics, and enjoy typing!

Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Mechanical Keyboard for Android.

Does Mechanical Keyboard record my passwords or credit cards?
No. The app does not implement a keystroke log or send typed text to developer servers. Android provides standard password protection for sensitive input fields, and all regular typing is handled locally on your device.
Can I silence the mechanical sounds when in a quiet meeting or library?
Yes! You can instantly mute or adjust sound levels directly from the companion app or the keyboard top toolbar with one tap, or have it follow your phone's vibration/silent mode.
How do I set my own photo as the keyboard background?
Open the Mechanical Keyboard app, enter the DIY Studio, and tap "Choose Photo" or "Camera". Select your favorite wallpaper, crop it to fit your layout, and adjust keycap opacity for optimal readability.
Can I easily switch back to my previous keyboard?
Yes. Android allows switching keyboards at any time by tapping the small keyboard switcher icon at the bottom of the navigation bar or via Android Settings > System > Languages & Input.
