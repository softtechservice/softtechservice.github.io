Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Hindi Calendar Panchang.

Does the app need an internet connection?

Yes. An internet connection is needed for precision astronomical ephemeris recalculations, cloud festival sync, and to load advertisements via Google AdMob. However, you can download supported monthly Panchang data to your device for offline reading during travel.

Is my Kundali birth chart data private?

Absolutely. All generated birth charts (Kundali), birth times, and birthplace coordinates are stored encrypted locally on your Android device. We never upload your personal birth details to our servers.

How do festival and Vrat reminders work?

You can set local notifications for upcoming festivals, Ekadashi, Pradosh, and Sankashti Vrats, as well as solar and lunar eclipses. Because notifications run locally on your phone, you never miss an auspicious occasion.

Why do festival dates sometimes differ by region?

Hindu festivals and tithis depend on the exact sunrise time at your location and whether local traditions follow Purnimanta or Amavasyanta month conventions. Our app clearly displays regional notes where observances differ.

Which Kundali layouts are supported?

You can choose between the classic North Indian diamond chart layout or the South Indian rectangular box layout, with support for both the Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) sidereal zodiac and the tropical zodiac.

Are there advertisements in the app?

Yes. Hindi Calendar Panchang includes non-intrusive advertisements served through Google AdMob. This allows us to keep the app free and continually update astronomical calculation engines.
