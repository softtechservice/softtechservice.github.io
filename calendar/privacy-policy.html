Welcome to Hindi Calendar Panchang ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed and published by SoftTech Service. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparent information practices.

This Privacy Policy outlines how our Android application collects, uses, handles, and protects information when you use our services, including daily Panchang calculations, the Hindu lunar calendar, festival notifications, Kundali generation, eclipse data, and associated services.

1. Information We Collect and Process

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Kundali Birth Chart Inputs: When you generate a birth chart (Kundali), you may provide a date of birth, time of birth, and birthplace / coordinates. This information is processed directly on your device and saved encrypted locally in your device's private storage. It is never uploaded to any external server or shared with third parties.
  • Custom Preferences & Settings: Chosen app language (from 9 Indian languages), visual theme (light / dark mode), preferred calendar week start, date & number formats, and saved favourite festivals or bookmarks.
  • Selected City / Region: Your selected geographical location for sunrise/sunset, tithi transitions, and local Panchang calculations.

Strict On-Device Encryption for Kundali: Your personal birth records and Kundali charts remain exclusively on your device, safeguarded by on-device encryption. You maintain full ownership and control over exporting or deleting your charts at any time.

B. Automatically Collected Information & Device Data

To deliver real-time celestial coordinates, ensure app stability, and serve contextual advertising, the app and our integrated SDKs automatically collect certain standard technical data:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version (Android OS), system language, screen resolution, and hardware architecture.
  • Network & Connectivity State: Connection status (Wi-Fi, mobile cellular data) to verify whether offline cache or live ephemeris data computation is needed.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad serving and frequency capping, subject to your Google account settings and consent choices.
  • Diagnostic & Crash Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic logs to identify unexpected crashes, calculation anomalies, or performance slowdowns.

We do NOT collect or store on our servers:

  • Your real personal name, telephone number, or email address.
  • Your contacts, SMS, audio recordings, camera, or personal documents.
  • Financial account numbers, bank details, or payment card data.
  • Continuous background GPS tracking traces.

4. Offline Access vs. Internet Requirement

Hindi Calendar Panchang is engineered with intelligent local caching:

  • Offline Availability: You can download and store supported monthly Panchang datasets, basic calendar dates, tithi markers, and saved festivals for convenient offline access during travel or weak network areas.
  • Internet Connectivity Requirement: An internet connection is required for detailed real-time planetary ephemeris recalculations, dynamic astronomical simulations, online regional synchronization, and loading AdMob ad inventory.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • Encrypted On-Device Storage: All created Kundali birth charts, personal notes, and favourite festivals are stored in local, sandboxed app storage using strong encryption.
  • No Central Server Retention: SoftTech Service does not operate centralized database servers that store, profile, or sell your birth data, charts, or personal notes.
  • Data Removal: Clearing app data or uninstalling the app permanently purges all locally stored Kundali records, preferences, and downloaded caches from your device.
