Capabilities

Designed for Fast Everyday Operations

Everything small shops, retail counters, freelance professionals, and service businesses need to run their daily billing smoothly without bloat.

Everyday Billing

Build clean, itemized bills with quantities, saved prices, discounts, and custom additional charges in seconds.

  • Flexible tax options: no tax, tax-inclusive, or tax-exclusive pricing
  • Configurable GST breakdowns (CGST, SGST, IGST) where required
  • Review totals before finalizing and record full, partial, or unpaid sales
  • Record Cash, UPI, Card, Bank transfer, and other payment modes
  • PocketBill records payment methods but does not process transactions

Products & Customers

Keep a comprehensive, searchable product catalog and customer ledger at your fingertips.

  • Categories, brands, item variants, selling prices, and product codes
  • Scan barcodes or use on-device OCR to capture product labels
  • Review text recognition scan results before saving into catalog
  • Bill with saved customers or perform quick billing without saving
  • View complete customer purchase history and outstanding dues

Bill History & Follow-Up

Search, filter, and maintain a complete audit trail of every transaction and customer payment.

  • Search saved invoices and filter by date, payment status, and amount
  • Record later payments as customer dues are collected
  • Review audited bill revisions and snapshot change histories
  • Duplicate existing bills into drafts or cancel and restore bills
  • Historical invoices preserve original business, customer, and item details

Invoices in Your Style

Create branded, professional invoices that represent your business identity with elegance.

  • Choose invoice layouts and adjust supported colors, alignment, and spacing
  • Customize shop logo, business address, header, and footer text
  • Generate print-ready PDF invoices and high-resolution invoice images
  • Share invoices directly through WhatsApp, Email, or messaging apps
  • Print via Android system print service with compatible thermal/POS printers

A Clearer View of Sales

Gain actionable visibility into revenue, customer payments, pending dues, and product performance.

  • View sales, recorded collections, and pending dues across date ranges
  • Track purchasing customer counts across selected business periods
  • Explore product sales activity and identify top-selling items
  • Follow customer balance trends and pending receivables
  • 100% computed on-device from your saved local database

Backup, Export & Portability

Safeguard your entire business ledger and export records whenever you need them for external bookkeeping.

  • Create a full local backup file for safekeeping and easy migration
  • Export bills, customers, and products as CSV files for spreadsheets
  • Safe restore routine: automatically creates a safety backup before restoring
  • Backups are not encrypted by PocketBill; keep backup files private
  • CSV exports are formatted for spreadsheets and cannot restore the app
Transparent Model

How Bill Credits Work

PocketBill uses a transparent, fair credit system designed for independent businesses. No recurring subscriptions or forced online accounts.

5 Starter Credits

Each store dataset starts with five bill credits, granted once upon initialization. You can start creating and testing invoices immediately.

1 Credit Per Bill

Issuing each new finalized bill uses one saved credit. Editing, viewing, or restoring an existing bill does not consume additional credits.

Earn via Rewarded Ads

Choose to watch an available rewarded ad online whenever you wish to earn more credits. Ads and rewards depend on network availability and completion.

Offline Guarantee & Grace

Saved credits can be spent completely offline. When credits reach zero, your draft is preserved and existing bills remain fully accessible.

Local Records & Practical Controls

PocketBill is engineered as an honest, self-contained business tool. Core billing, customer records, and invoice generation work completely offline. Rewarded ads and Google consent checks use the internet when you are online. Camera and selected-photo access support optional scanning and image attachment. Choose light, dark, or system appearance.

Tax Calculations

PocketBill supports customizable tax and GST breakdowns. Always review tax details before issuing invoices to customers.

Payment Recording

PocketBill records payment entries (cash, UPI, card, bank) but does not process monetary payments or handle card credentials.

Device Boundary

PocketBill does not synchronize records between multiple devices. Backups and exports are managed directly by you.

Printer Compatibility

Printing utilizes Android's system print service. Printing support depends on your device, installed print service, and printer hardware.
