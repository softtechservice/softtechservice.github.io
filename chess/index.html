✨ Game Features

A feature-complete classic chess environment built for casual play and strategy practice.

Classic Chess vs Computer

Full implementation of standard chess rules including castling, checkmate, stalemate, and pawn promotion.

Three Difficulty Levels

Choose from Easy, Medium, or Hard modes. The adaptive AI scales accurately to match your skill level.

Coin Rewards System

Earn coins for wins, draws, and every completed match. Build your coin balance as you perfect your moves.

Smart Move Hints

Stuck in a difficult tactical position? Watch a rewarded ad to trigger a smart move hint from the engine.

Classic Wood Theme

Warm walnut-and-cream board layout inspired by traditional real-life wooden tournament chess sets.

Audio & Vibration Settings

Fine-tune your audio settings. Easily mute sounds or toggle haptic feedback on board moves.

Seamless In-App Updates

Google Play flexible and immediate update flows deliver enhancements seamlessly without leaving the app.

Persistent Local Progress

Your coin balance, difficulty settings, and configuration details are securely saved directly on your device.

🛠️ Tech Stack

Engineered using Flutter and robust third-party mobile frameworks.

Flutter UI

Cross-platform UI rendered natively on Android platforms.

InAppWebView Engine

Bundled chess engine served locally via high-performance webview components.

Google Mobile Ads

Banner, interstitial, and rewarded ad units integration.

shared_preferences

Local key-value storage for settings, difficulty, and coin status.
