1. Overview

Chess Champion ("the App") is a single-player chess game developed by SoftTech Service. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. We do not require an account, login, or registration.

Local Storage: Game data such as your coin balance, selected difficulty, and sound/vibration preferences is stored only on your device using local storage (via shared_preferences) and is never uploaded by us. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

3. Advertising

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads). As part of serving ads, Google may collect and process certain data, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • App interaction data related to ad delivery and performance

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. You can learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner apps

You can reset or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Rewarded Ads

Watching rewarded ads (for example, to receive a move hint when you are stuck in a match) is always optional. Rewarded ads may use anonymous identifiers to personalize content, as described in Google's policies above.

5. In-App Updates

The App uses the Google Play in-app update service to check whether a newer version is available and to deliver updates. This process is handled by Google Play under Google's policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no chat, social features, or user-generated content. Advertising is served in accordance with Google Play's Families and Ads policies.

7. Data Security

Because the App does not transmit your game data to our servers, your progress, settings, and coin rewards remain on your device under your complete control.
