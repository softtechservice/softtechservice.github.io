Core Features

Everything You Need to Track Bullion

From live price boards to alerts, converters and widgets — Bullion Live gives you the tools market-aware users need.

Live Bullion Prices

Gold and silver boards, plus coin quotes in India. Local premiums, buy & sell spreads, today's high and low.

  • Gold, silver & coin quotes for India
  • Buy / sell prices and daily high–low
  • Per-gram, per-tola and per-troy-oz values
  • Search, sort and save favourite quotes
  • Offline mode shows last cached prices

Currency Converter

Convert amounts between supported currencies using live mid-market exchange rates. Perfect for comparing international spot prices.

  • Live mid-market exchange rates
  • International USD spot price conversion
  • Rates refresh periodically
  • Typed amounts stay private on device

Market Resources

Exchange and central-bank shortcuts for 20 countries, European resources grouped regionally, and quick links to economic calendars and market news.

  • 20-country exchange & central-bank links
  • European markets regional section
  • Economic calendar quick link
  • Market news shortcuts

Buy / Sell Calculator

Estimate a total by entering weight and choosing grams, tola, kilograms or troy ounces. Add margins, taxes and premiums. Save presets and recalculate with live prices.

  • Grams, tola, kilograms, troy ounces
  • Custom margin and tax / premium fields
  • Save presets for repeated use
  • Auto-recalculate with current live prices

Price Alerts

Set a price target above or below the current rate, or follow a new daily high or low. Review alert history and keep a persistent Android notification.

  • Target above or below the current rate
  • Follow new daily high or low events
  • Full alert history log
  • Optional persistent live-price notification

Widgets & More

Android home-screen widgets show 1–3 selected prices. Price history, share a price card, keep-screen-awake for shop counters and more.

  • Android widgets (1, 2 or 3 prices)
  • Price history: 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week
  • Share a price card instantly
  • Keep-screen-awake for shop counters
  • Dark and light themes, large-text support

Informational Use Only. Bullion Live displays live-market prices for information purposes. It does not provide trading, investment or financial advice, and does not buy, sell or store bullion. Prices are estimates and may differ from a dealer's final price. VAT/GST, duties and jewellery-making charges may apply and are not included unless the quoted source explicitly states otherwise. Always confirm prices directly with your local dealer. The app contains ads.

India & International Coverage

Bullion Live shows local bullion quotes for India. In supported countries outside India, it converts international USD spot prices into your selected currency using live exchange rates. Local premiums, taxes and duties may change the final displayed price.

Getting Started

Up and Running in Minutes

Install the app, choose your preferences, and get live gold and silver prices at your fingertips.

1

Install & Open

Download Bullion Live from Google Play. Open the app and select your preferred language, theme and price-refresh interval.

2

Favourite Quotes

Browse the gold and silver boards, star the quotes that matter most, and arrange them on your home screen widgets for a glance view.

3

Set Alerts & Presets

Configure price alerts for your target buy or sell price, save calculator presets with your margins, and let the app notify you when rates move.
