Official Privacy Policy, Terms of Service & License

Last Updated: 31 October 2026 Publisher: SoftTech Service contact@softtechservice.site

Summary

No account required. The app never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your settings, favourites, alerts, saved calculations and price history are stored only on your device.

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device identifiers and usage information to show and measure those ads.

We do not sell your personal information.

Information Stored on Your Device

To work, Bullion Live saves the following on your phone or tablet. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Preferences: language, theme, refresh speed and keep-screen-awake setting
  • Favourite quotes and custom sort order
  • Price alerts, alert history and quiet-hour settings
  • Calculator presets, margins and tax percentage
  • The most recent prices, so they can be displayed when offline
  • Prices recorded while you use the app, for up to seven days of price history
  • Widget and live notification settings

All on-device data stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Information Sent Over the Internet

Live Prices

The app requests prices and announcements from the bullion dealer's servers that publish the price feed. These requests contain no personal information from the app. As with any internet request, the server can see your device's IP address and basic technical details such as the time of the request. Prices are fetched over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection; dealer announcements may be fetched over HTTP and contain no information about you.

Currency Rates

When you use currency conversion or view non-India spot prices, the app requests exchange rates from its configured currency-rate provider. Requests identify currency codes only — the amount you type into the converter stays on your device. The provider may see your device's IP address and basic request details under its own privacy practices.

Market-Resource Links

Market resources open exchange, central-bank, calendar and news websites in your browser. Bullion Live does not send your saved app data to those sites. The sites and browser handle connection details under their own privacy policies.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads. The SDK collects and shares with Google information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising ID on Android
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about ads you see and tap
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Google uses this information to show ads, to measure them and to prevent fraud. On iOS the app does not ask for permission to track you, so Apple's advertising identifier is not available to it.

Your Ad Choices

Where the law requires it — for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom — the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → About & support → Ad privacy choices.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google or privacy settings.

Sharing & Background Updates

Sharing

When you share a price card or the app link, the image or text goes only to the app or person you choose in your device's share menu. Bullion Live does not transmit your data during sharing.

Notifications & Background Updates

Price alerts and the optional live-price notification are created on your device. When you enable the live notification or home-screen widgets, the app fetches prices in the background as described in the Information Sent Over the Internet section. You can turn these off at any time in Settings → Widgets & notifications or in your device settings.

App Updates & Ratings

When you check for updates or rate the app, Google Play handles the request under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your rating or account details.

Children

Bullion Live is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data Retention & Deletion

Information stored on your device remains there until you remove it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it permanently. We do not keep copies on any server. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

To delete all local data: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Bullion Live → Storage → Clear Data, or simply uninstall the app.

Security

The app does not transmit your personal information to us. Information stored on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we choose services and settings designed to protect your information.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live — for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU General Data Protection Regulation — you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you on our servers, most requests concern data held by Google; you can use Google's controls linked above. You can also contact us and we will assist.

Price Information Disclaimer

Prices in the app are indicative and may differ from final settlement prices. The app does not buy, sell or store metal and does not provide trading, investment or financial advice. Local taxes, duties, premiums and jewellery charges may not be included.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
