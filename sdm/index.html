Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about SDM capabilities and settings.

How does multi-threading speed up downloads?

SDM sends multiple simultaneous HTTP byte-range requests for different parts of the same file. This overcomes single-connection throttling from web servers and ISPs, maximizing your available bandwidth.

Can I download torrents without a tracker server?

Yes! With full support for DHT (Distributed Hash Table) and Peer Exchange (PeX), SDM can find swarm peers and download complete files without relying on a central tracker.

How does sequential piece downloading work?

Normally, BitTorrent downloads pieces in random order. Enabling sequential downloading forces SDM to prioritize pieces from the beginning of the file, allowing you to stream videos or audio while they finish.

What is Disk Pre-Allocation?

When a download starts, SDM pre-allocates the total required file space on your storage upfront. This prevents disk fragmentation on large files and prevents unexpected "Disk Full" errors midway through large downloads.

Does SDM track my downloaded files or URLs?

Never. SDM operates 100% on your device. There are zero analytical tracking SDKs, no external telemetry servers, and no user accounts. Your download links and history never leave your phone.

Is SDM really open source?

Yes, SDM is released under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later (GPLv3+), guaranteeing freedom of use, study, and improvement.
