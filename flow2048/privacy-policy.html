Legal Information & Policies

Last updated: 2026-07-03 | Developer: SoftTech Service

2048 Flow ("the game", "the app", "we", "our") respects your privacy. We are committed to protecting the integrity of your personal and game data. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled during your play sessions on the mobile application.

🔍 Information Handling & Data Collection

We believe in data ownership and privacy. The game operates under an **offline-first policy**, meaning all operations are performed locally on your device.

1. Personal Data

We do NOT collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • Account details or credentials.
  • Device-specific identifiers (such as IMEI or MAC address) on remote servers.
  • Contact lists, messages, photo galleries, or camera data.

2. Locally Persisted Game Data

To preserve your progression and achievements across sessions, the application writes metadata directly to local WebView database storage (IndexedDB/WebSQL). This includes:

  • Earned stars per level (100 total levels).
  • Global high scores and score records.
  • Total collected shop gold coins.
  • Status of unlocked profile avatars (Gamer, Neon Knight, Cosmic Explorer, Cyber Hacker).
  • Unlocked aesthetic grid themes (Classic, Neon, Galaxy, Cyberpunk).

Important: None of this local progress metadata is uploaded or synchronized with cloud infrastructure. If you clear the application's storage data or uninstall the app, this progress information will be permanently reset.

📱 Requested Device Permissions

To deliver an immersive hybrid gaming experience, the app requires standard device permissions. Below is a breakdown of their usage:

Permission Identifier Aesthetic & Functional Purpose
Vibration / Haptic API Synchronizes grid connection and tile merging feedback with native vibration motors.
Storage (Internal) Allows cache data storage and loading of WebGL assets (particle shaders, BabylonJS textures).
Network Access Required for downloading localized updates or redirecting to external stores for downloads.

🔐 Third-Party SDKs & Analytics

The game is designed without invasive ad trackers, telemetry integrations, or diagnostic SDK packages. We do not share telemetry details with advertising agencies or marketing partners.

👶 Children's Privacy Guidelines

2048 Flow does not collect any data, making it fully compliant with Children's Online Privacy Protection regulations (COPPA). We do not solicit data from individuals under 13 years of age.
