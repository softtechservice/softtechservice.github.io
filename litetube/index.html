Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how LiteTube works, what permissions it needs, and how it protects your resources.

LiteTube is engineered strictly around minimalism, performance, and flexibility. It delivers features that users love — such as floating Picture-in-Picture mini-player, background audio mode, offline downloads, and custom quality selectors — without unnecessary heavy frameworks, ads, or background tracking services.
Yes! LiteTube supports native Android Picture-in-Picture (PiP) so you can keep watching in a floating window while multitasking in other apps. It also supports background audio mode, allowing you to listen to podcasts, lectures, or music with your screen locked.
No account is required to start watching. You can jump right into the Home feed, search for videos, watch Shorts, browse Live TV, and create offline playlists. Your Continue Watching history and saved playlists are stored privately on your device.
LiteTube runs smoothly on Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above. Because of its lightweight architecture (<15MB installation package and low memory profile), it performs fluidly on both budget devices and flagship smartphones.
LiteTube includes an offline library where videos and media permitted by content sources can be saved to your device for viewing without an active internet connection.
